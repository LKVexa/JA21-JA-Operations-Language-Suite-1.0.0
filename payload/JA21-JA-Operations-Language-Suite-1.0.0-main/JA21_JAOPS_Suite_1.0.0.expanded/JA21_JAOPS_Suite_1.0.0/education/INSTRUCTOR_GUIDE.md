# Instructor Guide

## Teaching principle

Teach JA Operations as a language of bounded authority and reproducible state transitions. Do not grade visual polish or successful packaging alone. Grade explicit policy, deterministic inputs, recovery behavior, evidence linkage, and honest limits.

## Five-role review

- SOPHIA: semantic and systemic coherence
- CHARLOTTE: legible operational information architecture
- LANDON: compiler/runtime/platform feasibility
- Professor: invariant, counterexample, and test quality
- Podium: publication, integrity, and certification readiness

## Rubric

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Source and language correctness | 20% |
| Policy, effect, and capability correctness | 20% |
| Build/deployment/recovery design | 20% |
| Evidence identities and replayability | 20% |
| Diagnostics, limitations, and publication quality | 20% |

## Native evidence rule

The corpus contains modeled results. Students may cite them as specifications, but may claim native behavior only when they provide tool version, command, input hashes, outputs, and replay evidence.
